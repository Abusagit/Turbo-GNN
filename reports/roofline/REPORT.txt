Q3  WHAT THE MODEL GOT WRONG   -- measured traffic / compulsory traffic
================================================================================

  conv      mode         n    ratio    reuse   reading
  min_aggr  forward     32     0.36    2.81x   reuse: fetched less than compulsory
  min_aggr  backward    32     2.10    0.48x   amplification: fetched MORE
  gat_v2    forward     32     0.39    2.58x   reuse: fetched less than compulsory
  gat_v2    backward    32     1.19    0.84x   amplification: fetched MORE
  gt        forward     32     0.47    2.13x   reuse: fetched less than compulsory
  gt        backward    32     0.97    1.03x   matches compulsory

  Forward kernels fetch 36-47% of compulsory: the caches serve most neighbour reads,
  so modelled 'apparent bandwidth' overstated real utilisation by roughly 2.5x.
  min_aggr's backward fetches 2.10x compulsory: it scatters one atomic per
  (node, feature) through arg_idx, so 4-byte writes drag whole 32-byte sectors.
