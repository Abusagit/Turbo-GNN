# Stage-by-stage ablation, every graph

Baseline is `one_per_block` with sequential buckets. Each scheduling policy is shown
separately at that same baseline (sequential buckets, no edge slicing). `+streams` is the
best of {one_per_block, grid_stride} with concurrent light/heavy launches -- those are the
only two policies the stream sweep covered. `+edge slice` is the fully autotuned
configuration: scheduling, streams and the edge-sliced heavy bucket together.

Each cell shows milliseconds, and in parentheses the speedup against the baseline column.
